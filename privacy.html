Pikbot privacy policy

Pikbot is a personal tool with a single user: its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Pikbot accesses the owner's own Google account data, including Gmail messages and Google Sheets, in order to read transaction notification emails and update a personal spending tracker.

How the data is used

The data is processed on the owner's own computer and written to the owner's own Google Sheet. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Access tokens are stored on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer contact address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
